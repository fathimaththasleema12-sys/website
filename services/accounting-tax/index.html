Accounting & Tax Services

Maintaining accurate financial records is an important part of running a business. Our accounting and tax support can assist businesses with financial record organisation, accounting processes and applicable tax-related administrative requirements. The specific services required depend on the company's activities, structure and circumstances.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of the applicant/shareholder
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Company trade license, where applicable
  • Company incorporation documents
  • Bank statements or financial records, where applicable
  • VAT registration details, where applicable
  • Contact and address information

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss your business activity and accounting requirements with our team.
  2. Review your existing financial records and required documentation.
  3. Determine the appropriate accounting and tax-related services for your business.
  4. Organise and maintain the required financial records and documentation.
  5. Prepare applicable accounting or tax-related documents.
  6. Complete the agreed accounting and compliance support.

Important Information

Requirements can vary depending on the business activity, ownership structure, company size, applicable regulations and relevant UAE authority. The final document list and scope of accounting or tax services should be confirmed based on the individual business circumstances.

Power of Attorney

A Power of Attorney allows an authorised person to act on behalf of another person within the scope specified in the document. Requirements and procedures can vary depending on the purpose and type of Power of Attorney. NEXORA assists with document preparation, coordination and the applicable notarisation or authentication procedures.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of the person granting the Power of Attorney
  • Passport copy of the authorised representative, where applicable
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Details of the powers and authorities to be granted
  • Supporting company or business documents, where applicable
  • Contact and address information

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss the purpose and scope of the Power of Attorney.
  2. Review the required information and supporting documents.
  3. Prepare or coordinate the Power of Attorney document.
  4. Arrange the applicable notarisation or authentication procedure.
  5. Submit the documents and complete the required formalities.
  6. Receive the completed document and complete any related procedures.

Important Information

Requirements can vary depending on the type and purpose of the Power of Attorney, the parties involved, document language and the relevant UAE authority. The final document requirements and procedure should be confirmed before submission.

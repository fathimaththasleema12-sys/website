Corporate Support

Businesses often require ongoing administrative assistance after their initial setup. Corporate support can include help coordinating documents, renewals, government-related procedures and other administrative requirements. Our team provides ongoing assistance based on the specific needs of each business.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of the applicant/shareholder
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Company trade license, where applicable
  • Company incorporation documents
  • Business activity details
  • Existing company documents and records
  • Contact and address information

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss your business activity and ongoing support requirements.
  2. Review your existing company documents and administrative requirements.
  3. Identify the government, corporate or administrative services required.
  4. Prepare the necessary documents and applications.
  5. Coordinate the required procedures with the relevant authorities.
  6. Provide ongoing support for agreed corporate and administrative requirements.

Important Information

Requirements can vary depending on the business activity, ownership structure, company status and relevant UAE authority. The scope of corporate support and final document requirements should be confirmed based on the individual business circumstances.

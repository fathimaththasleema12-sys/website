Freezone Company

UAE free zones provide different options for entrepreneurs and companies looking to establish a business presence. Each free zone may offer different business activities, licence types, office solutions and ownership structures. We assist clients in understanding the setup requirements and coordinating the registration and licensing procedures for their chosen free zone.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of each shareholder
  • Passport-size photograph of each shareholder
  • Emirates ID copy, where applicable
  • UAE residence visa copy, where applicable
  • Proposed company or trade name
  • Details of the proposed business activity
  • Contact and address information
  • Additional documents required by the selected free zone

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss your business activity, ownership and company requirements.
  2. Identify a suitable free zone and licence option for your business activity.
  3. Choose an available company or trade name.
  4. Prepare and submit the required application and shareholder documents.
  5. Complete the applicable approvals, registration and licensing procedures.
  6. Complete any additional company, establishment or residency procedures required for the setup.

Important Information

Free-zone requirements, fees, licence categories, office requirements and approval procedures vary between authorities. The applicable requirements may also depend on the business activity, shareholder structure and nationality. The final document list and setup requirements should be confirmed with the relevant free-zone authority before submitting an application.

Legal Translation

Official documents may need to be translated depending on the requirements of the receiving authority or organisation. Our legal translation service helps clients arrange translations of relevant documents and prepare them for their intended official or business use.

Documents Required

  • Original document or clear copy for translation
  • Passport or identification document, where required
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Company or business documents, where applicable
  • Details of the required language pair
  • Contact and delivery information
  • Additional documents requested for the intended official use

How the Process Works

  1. Review the document and identify the required translation language.
  2. Confirm whether an official or legally recognised translation is required.
  3. Prepare and submit the document for translation.
  4. Complete the translation and required quality checks.
  5. Arrange certification or stamping, where applicable.
  6. Deliver the completed translated document for its intended official or business use.

Important Information

Translation requirements can vary depending on the document type, language, receiving authority and intended use. Some documents may require certified, sworn or legally recognised translation, as well as additional attestation or authentication. The final requirements should be confirmed with the relevant authority before proceeding.

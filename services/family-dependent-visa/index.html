Family & Dependent Visa

UAE residents may be able to sponsor eligible family members subject to the applicable requirements. The process can involve documentation relating to the sponsor and dependent, as well as entry permit, medical, Emirates ID and residency procedures where applicable. NEXORA assists clients with organising documents and coordinating the relevant application process.

Documents Required

  • Sponsor's passport copy
  • Sponsor's Emirates ID copy
  • Sponsor's UAE residence visa copy
  • Dependent's passport copy
  • Recent passport-size photograph of the dependent
  • Proof of relationship, such as marriage or birth certificate
  • Attested documents where required
  • Additional documents requested by the relevant authority

How the Process Works

  1. Review the sponsor and dependent eligibility requirements.
  2. Check the required documents and relationship evidence.
  3. Prepare and organise the application documents.
  4. Submit the applicable family or dependent visa application.
  5. Complete medical examination and Emirates ID procedures, where applicable.
  6. Complete the residency and government procedures.

Important Information

Family and dependent visa requirements can vary depending on the sponsor's residency status, relationship, dependent's age, nationality and the applicable UAE authority. Document attestation and additional approvals may also be required in certain cases. The final requirements should be confirmed before submitting an application.

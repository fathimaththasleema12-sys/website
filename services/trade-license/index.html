Trade License

A trade license is an important part of operating a licensed business in the UAE. The appropriate license depends on factors such as the nature of the business activity and the relevant licensing authority. Our service helps businesses understand the licensing process and coordinate applications, documentation and renewal procedures.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of the applicant/shareholder
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Proposed company name
  • Business activity details
  • Contact and address information

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss your business activity and requirements.
  2. Select the appropriate company structure.
  3. Choose and reserve an available trade name.
  4. Complete the required application and approvals.
  5. Prepare and submit the required documents.
  6. Complete licensing and government procedures.

Important Information

Requirements can vary depending on the business activity, ownership structure, applicant nationality and relevant UAE authority. The final document list should be confirmed before submitting an application.

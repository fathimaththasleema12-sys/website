Document Attestation

Certain documents may need to be authenticated or attested before they can be used for specific purposes in the UAE. The process can depend on the document type, country of issue and intended use. We assist clients in understanding the applicable attestation process and coordinating the required steps.

Documents Required

  • Original document requiring attestation
  • Passport copy of the applicant
  • Passport-size photograph, where applicable
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Details of the document's country of issue
  • Details of the intended use of the document
  • Supporting documents, where applicable
  • Contact and address information

How the Process Works

  1. Review the document type, country of issue and intended use.
  2. Confirm the applicable attestation requirements and procedures.
  3. Check the document and supporting documentation.
  4. Coordinate the required authentication or attestation steps.
  5. Submit the document to the relevant authorities or service channels.
  6. Complete the applicable UAE-side attestation or verification procedures.

Important Information

Attestation requirements can vary depending on the document type, country of issue, issuing authority and intended use in the UAE. Additional authentication, translation or supporting documents may be required in some cases. The final requirements should be confirmed before submitting the document.

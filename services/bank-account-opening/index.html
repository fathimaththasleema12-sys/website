Business Bank Account Opening

Assistance with corporate bank account applications and documentation for UAE businesses.

Bank Account Opening

Opening a corporate bank account can involve providing company documents, identification, business information and other information requested by the bank. We help businesses organise the required documentation and prepare for the application process. Final approval and requirements are determined by the individual bank.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of the applicant/shareholder
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Company trade license, where applicable
  • Company incorporation documents
  • Proposed company name
  • Business activity details
  • Contact and address information

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss your business activity and banking requirements with our team.
  2. Review the required company and personal documentation.
  3. Identify suitable banking options based on your business requirements.
  4. Prepare the required bank account application documents.
  5. Submit the application and supporting documents to the selected bank.
  6. Complete any additional requirements requested by the bank.

Important Information

Requirements can vary depending on the business activity, ownership structure, applicant nationality, source of funds and relevant bank. Bank account approval is subject to the individual bank's internal policies, compliance procedures and final approval. The final document list should be confirmed before submitting an application.

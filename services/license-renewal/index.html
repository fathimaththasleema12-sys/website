License Renewal

Business licenses generally need to be renewed according to the requirements and timelines of the relevant authority. Our team assists with the renewal process, helping organise the required documents and coordinate the applicable procedures. We also help businesses understand what information may be required before starting the renewal.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of the applicant/shareholder
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Existing trade or company licence
  • Business activity details
  • Contact and address information
  • Additional documents required by the relevant authority

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss your existing licence and renewal requirements.
  2. Review the current licence details and applicable renewal requirements.
  3. Confirm the required documents and information.
  4. Prepare and submit the renewal application.
  5. Complete the applicable government approvals and procedures.
  6. Complete the renewal and receive the updated licence documentation.

Important Information

Renewal requirements, fees, deadlines, penalties and supporting documents can vary depending on the relevant licensing authority, business activity and company structure. The applicable requirements should be confirmed before submitting a renewal application.

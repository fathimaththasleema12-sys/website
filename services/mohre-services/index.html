MOHRE Services

Assistance with employment-related procedures, documentation and services involving the UAE Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation.

MOHRE Services

MOHRE-related procedures can involve employment documentation, work-related applications and other administrative requirements. Our team helps businesses organise the necessary information and documentation and assists with the relevant procedures. Requirements can vary depending on the company, employee and type of application.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of the employee or applicant
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Labour or employment-related documents, where applicable
  • Company trade license and establishment information, where applicable
  • Employment contract or offer details, where applicable
  • Contact and address information

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss your company or employee requirements with our team.
  2. Identify the relevant MOHRE service and application requirements.
  3. Prepare and review the required employment and company documents.
  4. Complete the applicable forms and application procedures.
  5. Submit the required documents through the relevant channels.
  6. Follow up on the application and complete any additional requirements requested by the relevant authority.

Important Information

MOHRE requirements can vary depending on the employer, employee, employment category, nationality and type of application. The final document list and applicable procedures should be confirmed before submitting an application.

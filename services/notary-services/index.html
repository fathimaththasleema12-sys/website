Notary Services

Assistance with notarisation and preparation of documents requiring official notarisation procedures in the UAE.

Notary Services

Notarisation can be required for various agreements, declarations, powers of attorney and other legal documents. NEXORA helps clients understand the applicable procedure, organise the necessary documents and coordinate the notarisation process according to the requirements of the relevant authority.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of the applicant or concerned party
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • UAE visa copy, where applicable
  • Original document or draft document requiring notarisation
  • Power of attorney or authorisation documents, where applicable
  • Supporting company or business documents, where applicable
  • Contact and address information

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss the document and understand the required notarisation procedure.
  2. Review the document and identify the applicable requirements.
  3. Prepare the required supporting documents and information.
  4. Arrange the relevant notarisation application or appointment.
  5. Submit the documents and complete the required procedures.
  6. Receive the notarised document and complete any related formalities.

Important Information

Notarisation requirements can vary depending on the type of document, purpose of the notarisation, parties involved and relevant UAE authority. The final document list and procedure should be confirmed before submitting an application.

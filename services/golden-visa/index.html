Golden Visa

The UAE Golden Visa provides long-term residency options for eligible categories of individuals under the applicable UAE regulations. Eligibility and documentation depend on the applicant's category and individual circumstances. We assist clients with understanding the relevant requirements, organising supporting documents and coordinating the applicable application procedures.

Documents Required

  • Passport copy of the applicant
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Emirates ID copy, where applicable
  • Current UAE visa or residency details, where applicable
  • Proof of eligibility for the applicable Golden Visa category
  • Supporting certificates, qualifications or professional documents, where applicable
  • Investment, property or company-related documents, where applicable
  • Additional documents requested by the relevant UAE authority

How the Process Works

  1. Discuss your circumstances and determine the applicable Golden Visa category.
  2. Review the eligibility criteria and required supporting documents.
  3. Prepare and organise the required application documents.
  4. Submit the applicable Golden Visa application and supporting documents.
  5. Complete any required medical examination, Emirates ID or other applicable procedures.
  6. Complete the relevant residency and government procedures.

Important Information

Golden Visa eligibility and documentation requirements vary depending on the applicant's category, qualifications, investment status, property ownership and other applicable criteria. Additional approvals, attestations or supporting documents may be required. Eligibility should be confirmed with the relevant UAE authority before submitting an application.
